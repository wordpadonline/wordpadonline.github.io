How It Works

Getting started is simple. Follow these three easy steps.

1

Choose Your List Type

Select the type of list you want to create: To-Do, Shopping, Bucket List, Checklist, Wishlist, or Ideas. Each type is optimized for its specific purpose.

2

Add Your Items

Type in your items and optionally set the priority level (Low, Medium, High). Press 'Add Item' to include it in your list. Edit or delete items anytime.

3

Manage & Export

Check off completed items, download your list as a file, copy it to clipboard, or print it. Your data is always saved automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Online List Maker.

Is my data secure in Online List Maker? ▼

Yes! All your lists are stored locally on your device using browser storage. No data is ever sent to our servers or external services. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.

What types of lists can I create? ▼

You can create multiple types of lists including: To-Do Lists, Shopping Lists, Bucket Lists, Checklists, Wishlists, Ideas Lists, and custom lists. Each type is optimized for its specific use case.

Can I export my lists? ▼

Absolutely! You can download your lists as text files, copy them to your clipboard, or print them directly from the app. Click the 'Download', 'Copy', or 'Print' button to export your list.

Does the list maker work offline? ▼

Yes! Online List Maker works completely offline. You don't need an internet connection to use it. All your data is stored locally on your device and syncs instantly.

Will my data be saved if I close the browser? ▼

Yes! Your lists are automatically saved to your browser's local storage. When you close the browser or tab and return later, your lists will still be there exactly as you left them.

Can I set priorities for items? ▼

Yes! When adding items to your list, you can set the priority level as Low, Medium, or High. This helps you focus on what's most important.

Do I need to create an account? ▼

No account needed! Online List Maker works instantly without any signup. Start creating lists right away. All data is stored locally on your device.

Can I use this on my mobile phone? ▼

Absolutely! Online List Maker is fully responsive and works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops. The interface adapts to your device size for optimal usability.
