Frequently Asked Questions

Is Online Diary really free to use? ▼
Yes, Online Diary is completely free. No signup required, no login needed, and no hidden subscription fees. Write as many entries as you want, whenever you want, at no cost.
Is my diary completely private? ▼
Absolutely. Your diary entries are stored only on your device using your browser's local storage. They never leave your computer, are never sent to our servers, and are not tracked or monitored in any way.
Can I track my mood with my entries? ▼
Yes! You can select from six mood options: Happy, Sad, Neutral, Excited, Peaceful, or Angry. This helps you track your emotional journey and understand your mood patterns over time.
Is the diary saved automatically? ▼
Yes. Your diary entries are automatically saved as you type. You can also manually click the Save button anytime. Your data is never lost as long as you don't clear your browser data.
Can I use it on my phone or tablet? ▼
Absolutely! Online Diary is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. Write your diary entries on any device, anytime, anywhere.
How do I backup or export my diary? ▼
You can download your entry as a text file, copy it to clipboard, or print it. This ensures you always have a backup of your memories outside of your browser's storage.
What happens if I clear my browser history? ▼
If you clear your browser's cache and cookies, you may lose your diary entries. To prevent this, download your entries regularly as backup files to keep them safe.
Can I write multiple entries per day? ▼
Yes. You can write as many entries as you'd like. Each entry can have its own date and mood. Simply save one entry, then start a new one in the text field.
